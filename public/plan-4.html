4 | Early Risks, Opportunities and Quick Wins

Protect delivery now, and hold the bigger opportunities until the pipeline is complete

Guiding principle: Green Turf doesn't want dramatic change. The existing pipeline of mandates gets delivered first. Anything new is added only where it helps that delivery, and strategic change waits until the pipeline is complete. I've written this from outside the business, so I'd test it and adjust it in the first 30 days.

Risks

  • Change that distracts from delivery. This is the biggest risk I'd bring as a new starter. Mitigation: no new systems or restructures in 90 days, and Level 2 only where it helps live jobs
  • A committed job slipping on time, cost or quality. Mitigation: weekly live-jobs check-in and clearing blockers quickly
  • Over-committing: new work stretching current crews and plant. Mitigation: capacity check with Dean before new commitments
  • Cash gaps if milestones aren't invoiced promptly. Mitigation: invoice as each milestone is completed
  • Losing the team's trust by coming across as corporate. Mitigation: listen first, be on site, and help before suggesting anything

Quick wins

Only things that help the current pipeline, using existing tools:

  • Level 1 Clear the blockers job leads raise in week one, and let them know it's done
  • Level 1 Confirm tickets and inductions for everyone on live sites
  • Level 2 Invoice each milestone as it's completed and follow up overdue debtors weekly
  • Level 2 Capture variations on live jobs as they happen
  • Level 2 One short weekly check-in on live jobs, with owners and dates

Opportunities, once the pipeline is delivered

  • Level 3 Use actual costs from completed jobs to price future quotes more accurately
  • Level 3 Plan crews and plant ahead of the next pipeline
  • Level 3 Reduce manual admin, such as supplier invoice processing
  • Level 3 A simple KPI pack built from existing systems
  • Level 3 Grow recurring maintenance work off the back of completed jobs

Put forward at day 90. Mick and Dean decide the timing.

Examples of the later opportunities

To show how I'd approach the Level 3 opportunities, I put together three small prototypes on invented data. They aren't proposals for the first 90 days. They're examples of what could be considered once the committed pipeline is delivered, if Mick and Dean want to go down that path.

Level 3 Invoice reader →

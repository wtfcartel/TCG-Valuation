4 · Early Risks, Opportunities and Quick Wins

What could go wrong, and what we can win early

Plan for discussion. Written from outside the business, so every priority is a hypothesis I would test in the first 30 days, not a conclusion. The linked prototypes run on invented data.

Section 5 of 7 · use ← → keys to move between sections

Risks

  • Changing too much, too fast, or looking "corporate" to a hands-on team. Mitigation: listen first, start on site, small visible fixes
  • Key-person dependency on Mick and a few long-serving staff. Mitigation: document how the critical work gets done; build depth behind them
  • Growth outrunning capacity: quality, safety and reputation slip. Mitigation: monthly sales and capacity review
  • Cash squeeze from slow invoicing and upfront materials. Mitigation: 13-week cash forecast
  • Data may be thin or inconsistent. Mitigation: start with what exists; improve capture gradually

Quick wins (first 60 days)

  • Weekly margin snapshot on the five largest live jobs
  • Invoice-on-milestone rule and a weekly debtor call list
  • Ticket, licence and induction expiry register
  • One standard job handover checklist (sales → ops → site)
  • Fix one or two frustrations crews raise in week one, and tell them it was fixed
  • Weekly operations meeting with a visible action list

Opportunities (90 days +)

  • Use completed-job costs to price quotes more accurately
  • Automate supplier invoice processing and coding to cut admin time
  • Owned vs hired plant decisions from utilisation data
  • Grow recurring maintenance work for steadier revenue
  • A single KPI pack in Power BI or Excel from existing systems

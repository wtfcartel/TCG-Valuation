1 | How I Would Get to Know the Business

Learning the business through the committed pipeline

Guiding principle: Green Turf doesn't want dramatic change. The existing pipeline of mandates gets delivered first. Anything new is added only where it helps that delivery, and strategic change waits until the pipeline is complete. I've written this from outside the business, so I'd test it and adjust it in the first 30 days.

I'd get to know Green Turf through the work it's doing right now. That way I learn quickly, I don't pull people off delivery to brief me, and I can start being useful straight away.

Level 1 Start with the committed pipeline

  • Week one: sit down with Mick and list every committed job with its client, value, stage, deadline and owner
  • Agree what "complete" means for each: handover, client sign-off, final invoice, retention release
  • Walk each live job with the person running it: what's going well, what's at risk, what's in their way
  • Note the blockers I can clear without changing how anyone works

Level 1 People

  • Mick: the history of the business, what must not change, what he's concerned about and how he likes to be kept informed
  • Dean: what's been promised to clients, what's coming through the pipeline and what clients are saying
  • One-on-ones with managers, supervisors and the office team, fitted around their workload
  • Time on site with the crews, asking the same three questions: what works, what gets in your way, and what should I leave alone

Level 1 The numbers that already exist

  • For each live job: quoted versus actual cost to date, variations, invoiced and still to invoice
  • Cash: debtors, and when the committed work converts to cash
  • Safety: tickets, inductions and incidents on current sites
  • I'd use whatever reporting is already in place. Where a number doesn't exist, I'd note it for later rather than build something new mid-delivery

Level 3 Noted for later

  • Double handling, spreadsheets and manual approvals
  • Gaps in systems and reporting
  • Structure and capacity questions
  • These go on a list for the day-90 recommendations and aren't touched while the pipeline is being delivered

Output at day 30

A short note for Mick and Dean covering where each committed job is at, the risks to completing it well, what I'm doing to help, and what I've noted for later. We'd agree it together, and nothing material changes without that agreement.

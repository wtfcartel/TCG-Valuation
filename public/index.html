Green Turf | My First 90 Days

Business Operations Director | final-round presentation

Guiding principle: Green Turf doesn't want dramatic change. The existing pipeline of mandates gets delivered first. Anything new is added only where it helps that delivery, and strategic change waits until the pipeline is complete. I've written this from outside the business, so I'd test it and adjust it in the first 30 days.

Green Turf has an existing pipeline of mandates it has already committed to. My first 90 days would be about helping the team deliver that pipeline well, without dramatic change. Improvements come in small steps and only where they help delivery. Anything bigger gets prepared as options for Mick and Dean to consider once the pipeline is complete.

Deliver what's already been promised, protect its margin and cash, and only then look at what should change.

How the plan is sequenced

Level 1 Deliver the existing mandates

My first job. Every committed job in the current pipeline finishes safely, on time and at the margin it was quoted at. The only changes I'd make are to clear blockers on live work.

Days 1 to 90, and how I'd want to be measured.

Level 2 Light controls that support delivery

Only where they help the pipeline land and cost the team very little: a weekly check-in on live jobs, a margin snapshot, invoicing as milestones complete, and a ticket and induction register. Existing tools, no new systems.

Introduced gradually from about day 30, with the team.

Level 3 Strategic change, once the pipeline is delivered

Systems, automation, reporting, structure, capacity and growth planning. I'd prepare these as costed recommendations for Mick and Dean. Nothing here gets implemented in the first 90 days.

Put forward at day 90; the timing is Mick's call.

The 90 days at a glance

Days 1 to 30

Learn the business through the live work

  • Sit down with Mick and agree the list of committed jobs and what "complete" means for each
  • Meet every team, on site and in the office, and mostly listen
  • Understand each live job: where it's at, the risks, who owns it and what's in the way
  • Change nothing, other than clearing blockers on live jobs
  • Day 30: take Mick and Dean through what I've found and confirm the plan
Days 31 to 60

Get the pipeline over the line

  • Short weekly review of every live job with the people running them
  • Clear obstacles: materials, plant, crews, approvals, client decisions
  • Bring in Level 2 controls only where they help delivery
  • Invoice each milestone as it's completed
Days 61 to 90

Close out and recommend

  • Pipeline delivered, or on a clear and agreed path to completion
  • Capture the lessons from completed jobs while they're fresh
  • Write up the Level 3 options with costs and benefits
  • Day 90: review with Mick and Dean, and they decide what comes next and when

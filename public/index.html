Green Turf — My First 90 Days

Business Operations Director · final-round presentation

Guiding principle: no dramatic change. The pipeline of work Green Turf has already committed to is delivered first. Anything new is added only if it helps that delivery, and strategic change waits until the pipeline is complete. Written from outside the business, so it is tested and adjusted in the first 30 days.

Green Turf has a pipeline of work it has already committed to. My first 90 days are about helping the team deliver that pipeline well, without dramatic change. Improvements come in small steps, and only where they help that delivery. Bigger changes are prepared as options for Mick and Dean to decide on once the pipeline is complete.

In one line: finish what we have already promised, protect its margin and cash, and earn the right to suggest change by helping first.

How every part of this plan is sequenced

Level 1 Deliver what is already won

The first job. Help every committed job in the current pipeline finish safely, on time and at the margin it was quoted at. Changes are limited to clearing blockers on live work.

Days 1–90, and the measure of success.

Level 2 Light disciplines that support delivery

Only where they help the pipeline land and cost the team very little: a weekly check-in on live jobs, a margin snapshot, invoicing as milestones complete, a ticket and induction register. Existing tools, no new systems.

Introduced gradually from about day 30, with the team.

Level 3 Strategic change, later

Systems, automation, reporting tools, structure, capacity and growth plans. Prepared as recommendations for Mick and Dean, to decide on once the pipeline is delivered. Not implemented in the first 90 days.

Proposed at day 90; timing is Mick's call.

The 90 days at a glance

Days 1–30

Learn the business through its live work

  • Agree with Mick the list of committed jobs and what "done" means for each
  • Meet every team, on site and in the office; listen more than talk
  • Understand each live job: status, risks, owner, what is in the way
  • Change nothing, except clearing blockers on live jobs
  • Day 30: share what I found with Mick and Dean and confirm the plan
Days 31–60

Help the pipeline land

  • Short weekly review of every live job with the people running them
  • Remove obstacles: materials, plant, crews, approvals, client decisions
  • Introduce Level 2 disciplines only where they help delivery
  • Invoice each milestone as it completes
Days 61–90

Consolidate and recommend

  • Pipeline delivered, or on a clear track to completion
  • Capture lessons from completed jobs while they are fresh
  • Write up Level 3 options with costs and benefits
  • Day 90: review with Mick and Dean; they decide what, if anything, comes next and when

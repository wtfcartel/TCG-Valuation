Green Turf — My First 90 Days

Business Operations Director · final-round presentation

Plan for discussion. Written from outside the business, so every priority is a hypothesis I would test in the first 30 days, not a conclusion. The linked prototypes run on invented data.

Section 1 of 7 · use ← → keys to move between sections

Green Turf is a hands-on business that is growing. The job of a Business Operations Director in the first 90 days is not to arrive with answers. It is to understand how work really flows, protect what already works, and put in the few disciplines that let the business grow without losing margin, cash or people.

In one line: listen first, fix what is costing money or risking safety, and build an operating rhythm that frees Mick to lead and gives Dean confidence that what he sells can be delivered well.

The 90 days at a glance

Days 1–30

Listen and learn

  • Meet every team, on site and in the office
  • Follow real jobs from quote to cash
  • Baseline the numbers that exist, and note the ones that don't
  • Day 30: share what I found with Mick and Dean, and agree the priorities
Days 31–60

Prioritise and deliver quick wins

  • Start a weekly operations meeting with owners and dates
  • Weekly margin check on the largest live jobs
  • Tighten invoicing and debtor follow-up
  • Ticket and induction expiry register
Days 61–90

Embed and plan ahead

  • Monthly KPI pack that Mick, Dean and I all use
  • Monthly sales-and-capacity review with Dean
  • Business case for the one or two system changes worth funding
  • Day 90: review against the scorecard and agree the next 12 months

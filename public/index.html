Green Turf | My First 90 Days

Business Operations Director | final-round presentation

Guiding principle: Green Turf doesn't want dramatic change. The existing pipeline of mandates gets delivered first. Anything new is added only where it helps that delivery, and strategic change waits until the pipeline is complete. I've written this from outside the business, so I'd test it and adjust it in the first 30 days.

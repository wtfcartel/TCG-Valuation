5 · My Leadership Approach

How I lead, decide and earn trust

Plan for discussion. Written from outside the business, so every priority is a hypothesis I would test in the first 30 days, not a conclusion. The linked prototypes run on invented data.

Section 6 of 7 · use ← → keys to move between sections

People-centred, practical and calm. A growing, hands-on business needs a leader who is visible on site, clear about priorities, and steady when things go wrong.

How I lead

  • Listen before I act. The people doing the work usually know what's wrong
  • Be visible. On site regularly, not just in the office
  • Clarity. Every action has one owner and a date; every meeting ends with decisions
  • Fix the system, not the person. When something goes wrong, ask why the process allowed it
  • Coach. Build the next layer of leaders so the business doesn't rely on a few people

How I make decisions

  • Use data where it exists, judgement where it doesn't, and say which is which
  • Small, reversible changes first; bigger changes with a business case
  • Safety and integrity are never traded for speed or margin
  • Change my mind when the evidence changes, and explain why

What the team should say about me at day 90

"James listened, was on site, fixed things we'd been asking about for years, and we know what's expected of us."

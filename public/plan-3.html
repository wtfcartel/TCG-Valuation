3 · Partnering with the Owner/MD and the Marketing & Growth Director

How I would work with Mick and Dean

Plan for discussion. Written from outside the business, so every priority is a hypothesis I would test in the first 30 days, not a conclusion. The linked prototypes run on invented data.

Section 4 of 7 · use ← → keys to move between sections

The business works because of Mick, and it will grow because of the work Dean brings in. My job is to make both easier, not to add another layer.

With Mick (Owner/MD)

  • Understand what he wants to keep close and what he wants to hand over; write the decision rights down by day 30
  • Weekly one-to-one: no surprises, bad news early, options with a recommendation
  • Respect the culture he has built; earn the right to change things by delivering first
  • Free his time from operational firefighting for customers, strategy and the people he wants to develop

With Dean (Marketing & Growth)

  • Monthly sales-and-capacity review: pipeline against crews and plant, so we only promise what we can deliver well
  • Faster, more accurate quotes using real cost data from completed jobs
  • Feed delivery back into growth: completed-job case studies, photos, client feedback, repeat and maintenance work
  • Agree which work we want more of (by margin and fit), not just more work

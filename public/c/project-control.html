Early warning — would we have seen it in time? (P-101, monthly history)

What the numbers say

The amber trigger trips at the September month-end, when the project is 38% complete and 60% of the budget is still unspent — enough remaining cost to act on. By October the forecast margin is 13.2% against 20% quoted.

Two drivers are visible in the source data: plant hire is 44% over its budget once commitments are included, and a $85,000 subgrade variation has been worked but not approved. If that variation is approved in full, forecast margin moves to 16.9%.

Management action generated: variation submission escalated (Commercial Manager), plant-hire review against owned-fleet availability (see Demo 2), recovery plan from the PM within 2 business days.
